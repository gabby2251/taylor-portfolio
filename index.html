About me

Hi! I'm Taylor Pels-Hernandez, a marketing graduate of the University of North Texas who's all about creative strategy, authentic storytelling, and building meaningful connections. From running social media campaigns and organizing events to working with nonprofits and small businesses, I bring heart, hustle, and a whole lot of color to everything I do. I'm also a visual artist, mentor, and Fort Worth native who's passionate about using marketing as a tool to uplift others, tell powerful stories, and spark real change.

Artwork

the story behind it

My artwork is rooted in personal storytelling, emotion, and vibrant color. I use painting as a way to process the world around me, turning moments of grief, joy, and transformation into visual narratives.

Each piece I create is layered with intention, often inspired by the women who raised me, my cultural identity, and my experiences navigating life as a young Latina artist. I lean into bold colors and textures to evoke feeling and connection, inviting viewers into my world while also encouraging them to reflect on their own.

Art has always been my safe place. It's how I cope, celebrate, and communicate when words fall short.
